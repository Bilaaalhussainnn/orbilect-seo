# Dark city illustrations

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized and compressed with Sharp for this project. Outputs are 960×640 WebP files; these are stylised illustrations, not photographs.

## Statue of Liberty and compact New York skyline

File: `public/locations-dark-v11/new-york.webp`

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Website location card illustration, landscape 3:2. Subject: Statue of Liberty and compact New York skyline. A premium matte three-dimensional miniature architectural model on a floating dark plinth, midnight navy background #080b10, cobalt blue #004AAD accents and silver-grey architectural details, subtle technical grid and rounded browser-card silhouette behind model. Restrained soft lighting, clean composition with generous dark margin. Match a modern software and SEO website’s blue, grey and black 3D design language. No text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. One city scene only.
