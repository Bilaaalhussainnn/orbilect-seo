It works best when you can bring original data, a subject-matter expert, a newsworthy development, or a resource worth citing. Our team turns that material into a campaign and helps you reach people likely to find it useful.

## Measure the quality of coverage

We track where you were mentioned, who the publication reaches, what it linked to, and whether it sent relevant visitors. When tracking is available, we look at the leads or enquiries that followed.

Publishers control editorial coverage. We don’t buy links to influence rankings or promise placements. [Read Google’s guidelines on link spam](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).

## Frequentlyasked questions

### Before we pitch your story

See what we can prepare, what editors decide, and how we measure the result.

**Can you guarantee coverage or a backlink?**

No. Editors decide whether to cover a story and which links to include. We agree on the research, materials, outreach, and reporting we can deliver.

**Is digital PR the same as distributing a press release?**

A press release can provide facts for a timely announcement. Digital PR also includes research campaigns, expert commentary, resource outreach, and customized pitches for relevant audiences.

**How do you choose publications?**

We look at the topics they cover, their audience, geographic relevance, and recent editorial work. A smaller trade publication can be more relevant than a broad general-news site.

**What will I be able to measure?**

We report outreach, replies, published coverage, relevant mentions, and earned links. We use available analytics to check referral visits and conversions where attribution is reliable.
