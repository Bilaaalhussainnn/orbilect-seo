Before you pay for traffic

## What a profitable PPC campaign needs

Check the numbers and the customer journey before you set a budget. These are the questions we work through with you before launch.

**Know what a sale is worth**

Estimate what you keep after fulfilment and other variable costs, before ad spend. Use that contribution and your close rate to set a lead cost ceiling.

**Have an offer people can act on**

Give visitors a clear reason to choose you, a landing page that answers buying questions, and an obvious way to call, book, or buy.

**Track outcomes you can trust**

Test calls, forms, or purchase events. Decide how your team will mark qualified enquiries and reconcile them with sales.

**Budget for a useful test**

Allow enough room to test a focused market and collect data. Account for media, management, and any landing-page work separately.

**Be ready to fulfil and follow up**

Confirm stock, appointment capacity, and who responds to leads. Review missed calls and slow follow-up before adding spend.

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

Faqs

## PPC Managementfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**What does PPC management include?**

We audit or build campaigns, research keywords, write ads, review landing pages, verify conversion tracking, and optimize budgets and bids around agreed goals.

**Which paid search platforms do you manage?**

We work with Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising. We recommend where to start based on your audience, budget, and what you can measure.

**Is ad spend included in the management fee?**

We show platform spend and management fees separately in the proposal. That makes it clear what funds the ads and what covers our work.

**How much should I spend to start?**

We propose a test budget based on your locations, services, competition, and lead economics. We review the first results before recommending a larger commitment.

**Can you take over an existing PPC account?**

Yes. We review historical performance, existing campaigns, and tracking first. We keep useful account history and agree on the changes before making them.

**How do you reduce spend on irrelevant clicks?**

We review the search terms triggering your ads, add relevant exclusions, and adjust targeting when the data shows spend going to searches that don’t fit.

**What if we don’t have a dedicated landing page?**

We can assess an existing page or recommend a dedicated one. We agree separately on any copy, design, or development needed for the campaign.

**How do you tell a qualified lead from a form submission?**

We agree on criteria such as location, service need, and urgency. We compare tracked calls and forms with your team’s feedback and CRM data where available.

**How quickly can we judge PPC performance?**

We check tracking and search relevance soon after launch. A reliable cost per qualified enquiry takes enough conversion data to compare, so we set review points based on spend and volume.

**Does running Google Ads improve my organic rankings?**

Ad spend does not improve organic rankings. Paid search data can help us spot useful queries or landing-page gaps to address through separate SEO work.
