Hosting and migration

## When a slow website needs more than a redesign

An overloaded hosting plan can make pages slow or unreliable. Shoppers may leave before they see the offer, and recurring server errors can interfere with crawling. We find out where the delay starts before recommending a new plan.

[Discuss a redesign or hosting move](https://orbilectseo.com/#request-proposal)

1. ### Find the bottleneck

  Check server response times, mobile performance, uptime, and key pages under normal use. Slow hosting, oversized images, and heavy scripts need different fixes.
2. ### Choose the right upgrade or move

  If hosting is the bottleneck, we can help select a suitable plan or migrate to a new provider. For hosted builders and storefronts, we assess what can be improved in place and what would require a platform migration.
3. ### Protect the routes people already use

  Back up and test before cutover, preserve priority URLs when possible, map redirects when they change, and verify forms, checkout, HTTPS, and search monitoring after launch.

Our process

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

Faqs

## Website Redesignfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**Do I need a full redesign or just a few fixes?**

We review the pages and journeys causing the most friction. We may recommend targeted updates if the existing site can support them.

**How much does a redesign cost?**

Pricing depends on page count, content, integrations, migration, and support. We confirm what’s included and the price before starting.

**Will a redesign affect my existing SEO?**

A rebuild can affect visibility, especially when URLs or content change. We inventory important pages, map redirects, and verify key destinations after launch.

**Can you keep my existing branding?**

Yes. We can work with your current logo, typography, and colors, or apply a refreshed design if agreed in the scope.

**Do you handle copywriting?**

We can retain, edit, or rewrite priority copy as agreed. You’ll know which pages need input from your team before we publish.

**Can you integrate our booking tool or CRM?**

We assess what the tool supports and confirm how enquiries and confirmations should flow. Integrations are scoped before development.

**Will the site work on phones and tablets?**

We build responsive layouts and test priority pages and forms across representative screen sizes.

**Can I edit the site after launch?**

Where the build includes an editable CMS, we document what your team can change and explain how to make routine updates.

**What support is included after launch?**

The proposal specifies the support period, what fixes are covered, and how to request changes or ongoing maintenance.

**How do we know the redesign worked?**

We verify navigation, forms, and bookings at launch, then compare tracked enquiry and conversion data with the baseline when enough data is available.
