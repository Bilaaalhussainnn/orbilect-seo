Our process

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

Faqs

## Off-page SEOfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**What does Off-page SEO include?**

We review backlinks, citations, brand mentions, and review themes. The plan can include listing corrections, link reclamation, and editorial outreach.

**Do you sell packages of backlinks?**

We focus on relevant editorial opportunities and verifiable listings. Outreach and placements are reported individually.

**Can you guarantee a publication will link to us?**

Editors control what they publish. We agree on research and outreach deliverables and report actual responses and placements.

**Which directories should my business be on?**

We prioritize sources your customers use, such as credible trade associations, relevant local directories, and platforms where your category is active.

**What if a directory lists our old address?**

We document the conflicting details, confirm the correct information, and use the directory’s correction or ownership process where available.

**Can you recover a link lost after a redesign?**

We trace its old destination, identify the best replacement, and recommend a redirect or request an update when appropriate.

**Do you help with negative reviews?**

We identify recurring complaints, help draft appropriate responses, and suggest operational fixes for issues customers keep reporting.

**What do you need for digital PR?**

Useful inputs include original data, project details, specialist commentary, or a customer story you have permission to share.

**How do you report results?**

We report verified listings and placements, link destinations, and relevant referral activity when analytics is available.

**When should we review impact?**

Corrections and outreach can be reviewed early. We revisit referral traffic and enquiries after placements have had time to reach an audience.
