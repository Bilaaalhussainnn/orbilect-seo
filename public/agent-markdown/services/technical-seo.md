Our process

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

## Technical SEOfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**What is included in a Technical SEO audit?**

We review discovery, indexing, redirects, preferred URLs, sitemaps, and representative page performance. We prioritize findings by business impact.

**Can you fix the issues as well as audit them?**

Yes, when the stack and agreed access allow. We can also provide implementation tickets and verify work completed by your developers.

**Why isn’t an important page showing up in search?**

We check whether the URL is reachable, linked, indexable, and identified as the preferred version. Findings depend on the page and template.

**Do you need Search Console access?**

Access helps us inspect real URL and performance reports. We can start with public crawl checks and agree on access needed for deeper diagnosis.

**Can you help before a migration?**

Yes. We inventory priority URLs, prepare redirects, check staging, and define post-launch checks before changes go live.

**Do you work on JavaScript-heavy sites?**

We compare the HTML and rendered page, then check navigation, content, and metadata across representative routes.

**Can you improve Core Web Vitals?**

We identify likely causes and recommend template-level fixes. We verify what we can with lab checks and review available real-user data afterward.

**Do all crawl warnings need fixing?**

We prioritize issues that affect important URLs or user journeys. Intentional exclusions and alternate URLs often require documentation rather than changes.

**Will fixing technical issues guarantee rankings?**

Technical work clears specific obstacles. Search visibility also depends on the page’s relevance, competition, and demand.

**What do we get at handoff?**

You get prioritized findings, affected URLs or templates, implementation notes, and a record of what was verified.
