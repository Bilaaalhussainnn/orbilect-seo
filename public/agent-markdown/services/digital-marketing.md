Our process

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

Faqs

## Digital Marketingfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**Which digital marketing channels do you recommend?**

We start with where customers are likely to look and what your team can measure. That may mean search, paid social, email, or a focused combination.

**Is ad spend included in management fees?**

We show media spend and management fees separately in the proposal so you can see what goes to platforms and what covers the work.

**Can you audit our existing ad account?**

Yes. We review structure, search terms, conversion tracking, landing pages, and budget allocation before recommending changes.

**Do you build landing pages?**

We can recommend or implement landing-page improvements when included in the scope. Larger builds are quoted separately.

**What counts as a qualified lead?**

We agree on criteria with you, such as service need, location, or budget. Your team’s feedback helps validate lead quality.

**How much should we spend to start?**

We propose a test budget based on the audience, offer, market, and measurement plan. We review the data before recommending a larger commitment.

**Can you run ads while we improve SEO?**

Yes. We can use paid campaigns to test terms and offers while improving pages that serve organic customers.

**How do you attribute calls and forms?**

We use agreed tracking tools and events where access allows. Reports identify gaps when a conversion cannot be tied confidently to a channel.

**Do we keep access to our accounts?**

We work with access to accounts you control where possible. Ownership and permissions are confirmed during onboarding.

**When do you change or scale a campaign?**

We review search terms, conversion quality, and costs. We adjust when the data supports it and document the reason.
