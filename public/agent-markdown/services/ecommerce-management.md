AI agent comparison

## AI chat agent or agentic AI?

An AI chat agent supports a conversation. An agentic AI system can also do approved work across your operations tools.

| Capability | AI chat agent | Agentic AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Answers questions and guides customers. | Completes an approved operational task. |
| Trigger | Usually starts with a live chat message. | Can start from a message, event, or schedule. |
| Tools | Reads the knowledge base and approved lookups. | Coordinates multiple approved systems through APIs. |
| Steps | Follows conversation and handoff rules. | Plans and executes several steps inside a defined workflow. |
| State | Carries context within the conversation. | Can track progress on a task after the conversation ends. |
| Actions | Explains policy or prepares a handoff. | Can write to approved systems or queue an action for review. |
| Approvals | Escalates requests beyond the chat scope. | Pauses before sensitive actions such as refunds or account changes. |
| Errors | Hands unresolved questions to an agent. | Uses bounded retries, records failures, and routes exceptions. |
| Measurement | Answer accuracy, resolution, and handoff quality. | Task completion, error rate, and approval turnaround. |
| Example | Explains how to track an order. | Checks the carrier, creates a ticket, and queues a follow-up. |

## Frequentlyasked questions

### Before we take on your store

Find out what we can manage, how we work with your team, and who stays in control of your accounts.

**Can Orbilect SEO manage the entire store?**

Yes. We can combine brand and store design, ecommerce SEO, paid media, and day-to-day store operations in one scope. We agree which responsibilities stay with your team or your fulfillment partner.

**Can you work with a store that is already live?**

Yes. We can improve the store in place or plan a migration. We start by identifying what already generates sales and protecting important URLs, tracking, and checkout flows during changes.

**Do you work with stores selling to more than one country?**

Yes. We plan around the markets you sell to, including language, currency, delivery, returns, and compatible payment options. The exact setup depends on the platform and providers.

**Who controls the store, ad accounts, and refunds?**

You keep ownership of your business accounts. We use permission-limited access and follow the approval rules you set for ad spend, cancellations, refunds, and changes to the live store.

**Can a chatbot replace ecommerce customer support?**

It can answer routine questions from approved information and help route tickets. We define when it hands over to a person, especially for disputes, payment or account questions, and exceptions.

**Can we start with only one part of ecommerce management?**

Yes. Start with a scoped launch, a growth program, or an operating workflow. We can add other responsibilities when the store and your team are ready.

**What does ecommerce customer support handle?**

Our agents can answer product and availability questions before a sale, help with checkout and delivery options during the sale, and support order updates, returns, and escalations afterward.

**Can you build an AI chatbot for my online store?**

Yes. We can build a chatbot that uses approved product, shipping, and returns information. We define what it can look up and when it hands off to a person, including refund or account decisions.

**How are ecommerce customer support agents trained?**

We assess typing speed and accuracy, interview candidates, and train selected agents on your products, policies, and tools. Agents practice repeated mock chats and calls, receive coaching, and are assessed before handling live queues.

**Which platforms do you build and manage ecommerce stores on?**

We can build and manage stores on Shopify, WordPress with WooCommerce, Magento or Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, and Webflow. We scope design, migration, inventory, and integrations around the platform and markets you use.

**Can you build a custom CRM for an ecommerce store?**

Yes. We can scope a dedicated CRM or integrate your existing tools so permitted staff can see customer history, orders, stock, returns, and support cases in one place.

**What can agentic AI automate in ecommerce?**

Agentic AI can carry out approved multi-step tasks such as checking a delayed order, opening a ticket, flagging stock mismatches, or assigning follow-ups. We define tool permissions, approval steps, and escalation before rollout.

**What is the difference between an AI chat agent and agentic AI?**

An AI chat agent answers questions and routes conversations. Agentic AI can also act across approved tools, keep track of a task, request approval, and report outcomes. The comparison covers ten differences and an order-tracking example.

**What are the access and approval rules for ecommerce support and automation?**

You control business accounts and grant only the permissions needed for the work. We agree which actions require approval, including refunds, spending, and sensitive account changes, and route exceptions to a person.

**Where can I read your privacy policy for ecommerce support and AI data?**

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle contact details, project access, and Naila conversations. For ecommerce support or AI projects we agree what customer data the team and tools may access before work starts.

**Where can I read billing and notice terms for offshore customer support?**

The Terms and Conditions explain staffing billing and notice. Your signed staffing agreement confirms the roles, coverage, fees, notice arrangements, and handover that apply to your engagement.
