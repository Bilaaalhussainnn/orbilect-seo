We work within Shopify’s catalogue and theme structure. The priority is to make collections useful search destinations and keep product details consistent across the storefront.

**Map demand to collections**

Build collection pages around what shoppers compare, such as waterproof hiking boots or wide-fit running shoes. Use clear titles, helpful copy, and links to in-stock products.

Review the theme’s product markup against visible price, availability, and variants. Check whether apps are duplicating markup or slowing down key templates.

**Make the sitemap useful**

Review Shopify’s generated sitemap, submit it in Search Console when needed, and inspect the collections and products that matter most.

**Connect visibility to sales**

Compare organic impressions and clicks with tracked landing-page visits and orders. Account for promotions and stock changes when reviewing results.

## Ecommerce SEOfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**What does Ecommerce SEO cover?**

We work on collections, product pages, internal links, filters, variants, and relevant technical checks. We prioritize the catalogue around your business goals.

**Can you work on Shopify or WooCommerce?**

Yes. We review the platform and theme before agreeing on changes, including which edits your team or developer needs to make.

**Should we optimize products or collections first?**

It depends on how shoppers search. Broader demand often needs a collection, while a specific model or product name usually needs a product page.

**Can you help with duplicate product URLs?**

We trace variants, filters, and collection paths, then recommend which URLs should be preferred and how navigation should point to them.

**What should we do when a product is out of stock?**

We consider whether it will return, whether it has a useful replacement, and what visitors need to know before recommending a page change.

**Do we need to rewrite every product description?**

We start with priority products and reusable template improvements. Original fit, compatibility, and use details often matter more than adding length everywhere.

**Does product schema guarantee enhanced search results?**

It can help eligible pages qualify for supported search features. The search engine decides what to display.

**Can you preserve visibility during a store migration?**

We map important category and product URLs, verify redirects, check key templates, and monitor the new storefront after launch.

**How do you measure the work?**

We review relevant impressions, clicks, organic landing pages, and tracked orders or revenue, taking stock and promotional periods into account.

**Do you also help with Shopping ads?**

Yes, when campaign management is in scope. We track paid media separately and can use campaign insights to inform collection and product priorities.
