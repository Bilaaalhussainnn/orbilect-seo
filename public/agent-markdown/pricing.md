Payment structure

## When each payment is due

We quote fixed-price projects and recurring services separately. Before work begins, we set out the service terms, deliverables and billing in the signed service agreement. Both parties sign the NDA covering confidentiality and access.

Category 1

### Fixed-price projects

Standard fixed-price projects usually require a 40% advance before work begins. It reserves specialist time and is non-refundable except where applicable law requires a refund, a billing error needs correction, or the parties agree otherwise in writing.

The remaining balance, usually 60%, is due when we deliver the final draft for review and before we hand over the project credentials or agreed ownership access. Existing client-owned accounts remain under the client’s control.

Category 2

### Recurring services

The next recurring invoice is due 30 calendar days after the initial payment. Later invoices follow the same 30-day schedule, regardless of the number of days in the month.

Where available, you can authorize direct debit for the scheduled calendar date. See your invoice for the payment method and due date.

### Promotional purchases

Promotional offers are payable in full and non-refundable, subject to applicable law and correction of billing errors. See the terms for refunds and staffing notice periods.

## Pricing and refundsfrequently asked questions

### Before we start your project

Find out which pages we work on, how changes get published, and how we measure the result.

**How much do Starter and Essential cost?**

Regular Starter starts at $650 per billing cycle. Essential is $1,500 per billing cycle. We quote Custom plans after reviewing the work you need.

**What’s included in Essential?**

Essential includes website redesign within the agreed scope, 12 months of complimentary website bug support, hosting migration, eight blog posts per month, relevant business directory profile setup or cleanup, and ongoing SEO reporting.

**How do you calculate a custom quote?**

We review your starting point, the work in scope, the specialists and hours required, and the support you need. The proposal sets out the deliverables, schedule, and billing.

**Which services are one-time and which recur?**

Starter and Essential are recurring plans billed every 30 calendar days. Ongoing PPC or social media management can also be quoted monthly. Standalone redesigns, migrations, and defined projects can be quoted as one-time work.

**Does PPC management include ad spend?**

Media spend and campaign management are quoted separately. The proposal identifies the agreed management work and the budget allocated to the advertising platform.

**Will I see what I’m paying for on the invoice?**

Yes. Your invoice identifies the agreed services, whether each item is one-time or recurring, and any separate charges that apply.

**Does the free audit commit me to a paid plan?**

You can request the free audit and review the recommendations before deciding whether to request a quote for paid work.

**How do I request a project refund?**

For eligible standard fixed-price projects, email [refunds@orbilectseo.com](mailto:refunds@orbilectseo.com) within 14 calendar days after we hand over the project credentials. We review the work against the agreed scope and may refund up to the amount paid above the advance, usually the 60% balance. Legal rights and any written exceptions still apply.

**Can I cancel recurring services or staffing?**

Give at least one month’s written notice. For staffing, we refund unused prepaid months after the effective end date without a separate early-exit penalty. Send refund requests to [refunds@orbilectseo.com](mailto:refunds@orbilectseo.com).

**Are promotional purchases refundable?**

Promotional offers are payable in full and non-refundable, subject to applicable law and correction of billing errors.

**How long does an approved refund take?**

We initiate approved refunds within 14 calendar days of written approval, or sooner where required by law. Your bank or payment provider may take additional days to show the credit.
