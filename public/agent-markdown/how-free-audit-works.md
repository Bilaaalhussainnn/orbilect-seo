How the audit works

## From your website to a plan of action

Your report includes an executive summary, evidence from the review, affected pages where identifiable, and recommended fixes in priority order. We flag findings that need account access or further investigation.

2. ### We review the site

3. ### Get a prioritized report

Why an audit matters

## Find the issue before you spend time fixing it

An audit helps us pinpoint what’s holding your site back and focus your team’s time and budget on the work that matters most.

### Find the cause before starting the fix

An audit connects each issue to the pages it affects. We look at the evidence to understand whether a page is hard to find because of indexing, weak internal links, or content that misses what customers searched for.

**Example**

If an important service page is blocked from indexing, we check why before spending time rewriting it.
