Offshore staffing contracts

## From your brief to your first working day

Orbilect SEO runs the hiring process. Your company makes the final selection and works directly with the specialist on the day-to-day brief.

1. Brief

  ### Send us your role brief

  Tell us what the specialist needs to do, which tools they’ll use, and the working hours and time-zone overlap you need. We agree on the scope, fees, and hiring plan.
2. Sourcing

  ### We research and post the role

  We research the talent market, draft the job description, and advertise through relevant channels. We review applications against the agreed criteria.
3. Assessment

  ### We examine, test, and interview

  We screen experience and availability, give qualified applicants a role-specific practical test, and interview them about how they approached the work.
4. Shortlist

  ### You choose from the shortlist

  We share relevant profiles and assessment summaries. You can interview the finalists and make the final selection.
5. Contract

  ### We agree the contract and onboard

  The signed agreement identifies the employing or contracting party and sets out fees, coverage, responsibilities, confidentiality, and the notice or replacement terms. We then confirm a start date and onboard the selected specialist.
6. Start

  ### Work directly with your specialist

  Your specialist works from our office and communicates directly with your team about priorities, approvals, and progress. They use approved workstations and only the accounts and permissions you authorize.

## Staffingfrequently asked questions

### Before you hire

Find out how we shortlist candidates, what your quote covers, and how your specialist works with your team.

**What roles can Orbilect SEO help us fill?**

We source SEO specialists, writers, paid media and social media marketers, designers, developers, analytics specialists, and digital operations staff. Share what you need done and we’ll help define the role.

**How much does a dedicated offshore specialist cost?**

Your quote depends on the role, experience level, working hours, and coverage. We confirm the fee, what it includes, and how it’s billed before you commit.

**How long does it take to find someone?**

Timing depends on the skills you need, candidate availability, and how quickly interviews and approvals can happen. We agree on an estimated schedule after reviewing your brief.

**How do you screen and shortlist candidates?**

We research the market, post the role, review applications, assess practical skills, and interview suitable candidates. You receive a shortlist with relevant assessment notes.

**Can we interview candidates and make the final choice?**

Yes. You can interview the finalists, ask them about their work, and decide who joins your team.

**Can our specialist work during US business hours?**

Tell us your time zone and how much overlap you need. We confirm the schedule and candidate availability before you make a selection.

**Where will the specialist work?**

Your specialist works from our office using an approved workstation. Personal electronic devices, pens, and paper are prohibited in the work area.

**Can we work directly with our specialist?**

Yes. Your designated team can communicate directly with the specialist about priorities, feedback, and approvals. We agree on the channels and working routine during onboarding.

**How do we control access to our tools and client data?**

You authorize the accounts and permissions needed for the role. We agree on any handling restrictions before work starts and coordinate access removal when it’s no longer needed.

**What if the specialist isn’t the right fit or we need to end the engagement?**

Tell us what isn’t working so we can review the issue with you. Any replacement, notice, billing, and handover arrangements follow the signed staffing agreement.

**What customer support roles can I offshore?**

For businesses looking for affordable offshore support, we can recruit ecommerce customer support agents for live chat, email, phone, multilingual coverage, or a multichannel role. We can also scope an AI chatbot and its handoff to human agents.

**How much does offshore customer support cost?**

Orbilect quotes offshore support based on skills, languages, shifts, tools, and supervision. On the staffing page you can propose an hourly budget for a human agent and send it to our team for review. AI projects use a separate scope without an hourly staffing budget.

**Can an offshore customer support agent work in more than one language?**

Yes. Tell us the markets, languages, channels, and hours you need. We assess written and spoken fluency for the role and confirm candidate availability.
