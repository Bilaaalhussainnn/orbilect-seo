Hiring process

## From application to offer

Here’s what to expect when a position opens. The vacancy listing will explain how to apply and which steps are relevant to the role.

### Where to apply

Check Current Openings for the application link. For roles that accept email, attach your résumé and include the position you’re applying for.

[careers@orbilectseo.com](mailto:careers@orbilectseo.com?subject=Orbilect%20SEO%20job%20application)

1. **Submit your application**

  Apply through the vacancy listing on our website. If the listing accepts email applications, send your résumé to careers@orbilectseo.com and put the role title in the subject line.
2. **Initial screening call**

  We discuss your experience, availability, and interest in the role, and answer your initial questions.
3. **First interview**

  Meet the hiring team to talk through your experience, how you approach the work, and what the role involves.
4. **Role-specific assessment, if needed**

  Some roles include a practical assessment. If one applies, we’ll explain the task, the time involved, and what we’ll evaluate.
5. **Final interview**

  Discuss responsibilities, team expectations, and any remaining questions with the hiring lead.
6. **Background and qualification checks**

  We explain which checks apply to the role. These may include verifying your degree, employment history, professional references, or other relevant credentials.
7. **Written offer and follow-up**

  If selected, you’ll receive a written offer detailing your role, department, designation, salary, other compensation and benefits, start date, and employment terms. We’ll follow up to discuss the offer and answer questions before you decide.
