Our process

## How we work

From the first conversation to kickoff, you’ll know what we recommend, what it costs, and how we’ll measure progress.

1. Discovery

  ### Discovery call

  We start by understanding your business, who you want to reach, and what you want your website to do better. We’ll discuss your products or services, target markets, current SEO efforts, and the outcomes that matter to you, such as qualified enquiries, bookings, or product sales.
2. Confidentiality

  ### Confidentiality and NDA

  We agree on how confidential information will be handled and sign an NDA before exchanging sensitive materials. We also identify which reports and accounts are relevant to the engagement and agree how to share access with the people who need it.
3. Assessment

  ### Initial SEO assessment

  We review how search engines discover your website, which pages currently attract visitors, and where competitors have stronger coverage. That gives us a starting point for prioritizing technical fixes, keyword opportunities, and pages that could better serve your customers.
4. Proposal

  ### Proposal and scope

  We turn the assessment into a proposal that spells out the recommended work, deliverables, timeline, and pricing. You can review what we’ll tackle first, ask questions about the trade-offs, and approve the scope before we schedule kickoff.
5. Invoice

  ### Invoice and payment

  Once you approve the scope, we send an invoice that reflects the agreed work and payment terms. After payment is confirmed, we coordinate the kickoff date and share what we need from your team to get started.
6. Kickoff

  ### Official kickoff

  At kickoff, we confirm access, review the agreed priorities, and record a baseline for organic visibility and relevant traffic. Then we begin the work, agree on how updates will be shared, and use that baseline to track progress over time.

### What Orbilect SEO audits and improves

**Technical SEO and indexing**

- Fix broken internal links, 404 errors, and redirect chains. Map moved URLs to relevant destinations.
- Review robots.txt for crawl access, noindex directives for index eligibility, and canonical tags for duplicate URLs.
- Clean up XML sitemaps and validate relevant Schema.org markup for products, businesses, and breadcrumbs.

**Website design and page structure**

- Improve navigation, page hierarchy, URLs, headings, and internal links so key pages are easier to find.
- Review mobile layouts, page speed, and Core Web Vitals. Optimize images and address heavy scripts or layout shifts.
- Make service, product, and location pages easier to read and give visitors a clear next step.

**On-page content and blogs**

- Match pages to search intent and refine titles, descriptions, headings, and image alt text.
- Publish blogs that answer questions customers ask before buying, such as cost, comparisons, and how to choose a provider.
- Link useful articles to relevant service or product pages and refresh outdated content.

**Guest posts and digital PR**

- Review existing backlinks and identify relevant publications, industry directories, and partners.
- Pitch original guest articles, expert commentary, or useful resources to publications your customers read.
- Measure referral visits and relevant coverage. Disclose sponsored placements and qualify paid links.

**Social media and content distribution**

- Turn guides and reports into posts that help people discover the original content.
- Share useful posts where your customers already spend time and track visits from each campaign.
- Use audience questions and feedback to identify topics for future pages, guides, and updates.

**Local SEO and reporting**

- Review Google Business Profile, Bing Places, location pages, and business details across relevant listings.
- Monitor indexing, impressions, and clicks in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Track calls, enquiries, or sales where measurement is available and use the findings to prioritize the next round of work.

## Frequentlyasked questions

### What to expect from Orbilect SEO

Get clear answers on pricing, timelines, and how we work. If you’re wondering where your own website stands, start with a free audit.

**How long does SEO take to show results?**

It depends on your starting point, competition, and how quickly changes go live. We use the audit to identify what to fix first and agree on what we’ll measure over time.

**When will I start seeing changes?**

You can review updates to pages and content as soon as they go live. Search engines need time to revisit those pages. We look for changes in indexing and search impressions before judging trends in traffic, enquiries, or sales.

**How much does Orbilect SEO cost?**

Regular Starter SEO starts at $650 per billing cycle. Published promotional offers have separate eligibility and terms. Your quote depends on the size of your website, the markets you serve, and the work needed. Your proposal outlines the scope, deliverables, and fees.

**What is your refund policy?**

Refund eligibility depends on the terms in your signed agreement. If you want us to review a payment, send us the invoice number and a brief description of the issue.

**What services does Orbilect SEO offer?**

We offer on-page SEO, off-page SEO, technical SEO, ecommerce SEO, digital marketing, and website redesign. On-page SEO can include keyword research, SEO content writing, metadata, and internal linking.

**What does the free SEO audit cover?**

We review your website for search visibility gaps, technical issues, and on-page improvements. We flag the fixes we’d prioritize and explain why they matter for your business.

**Do you guarantee a #1 Google ranking?**

Search rankings vary by query, location, competition, and the results Google chooses to show. We agree on practical targets and track progress through search visibility, relevant traffic, and conversions where tracking is available.

**Can you help with Shopify and other ecommerce websites?**

Yes. We can review product pages, collections, site navigation, and technical issues such as duplicate URLs. We prioritize changes that help shoppers find the products they’re looking for.

**Can you work with our existing website and team?**

Yes. We can coordinate with your developers, writers, or marketing team and agree on who implements each change. If a redesign is part of the scope, we plan for URL redirects and existing pages that already attract search traffic.

**How will I know if the work is paying off?**

We start with a baseline and agree on the metrics that matter to your business. Reporting can cover search impressions, clicks, priority pages, and leads or sales where tracking is available.
