## Frequentlyasked questions

### What automotive teams ask us

Get answers about inventory feeds, local search, service bookings, and parts catalogs. Start with a free audit to see where your website can improve.

**What does automotive SEO include?**

It covers the pages customers use to choose a vehicle, book a repair, or buy a part. We review technical SEO, local visibility, content, and how easily visitors can take the next step.

**Can you optimize a dealer site that uses a third-party inventory feed?**

Yes. We start by checking which feed and template settings you control. We separate changes your team can make from changes that require your website provider.

**What should happen when a vehicle is sold?**

We review the listing’s search value and available alternatives. Depending on the page, we may keep it accessible with clear sold status, direct visitors to a close alternative, or retire it cleanly.

**Can you help a dealership with multiple locations?**

Yes. We plan pages around each location’s actual inventory, services, and hours, then check that business profiles and enquiry links point to the right branch.

**What should an independent repair shop prioritize?**

Start with the repairs you want to book and the areas you serve. We make those pages clear about the work you do, the vehicles you handle, and how to get an appointment.

**How do you approach SEO for parts and accessories?**

We review categories, part numbers, fitment content, and filters so buyers can confirm compatibility and reach the right product. We also check how the site handles unavailable items.

**Can you track test drives, phone calls, and service bookings?**

We can set up or review measurement for those actions, subject to access and the tools your site and booking provider support.

**Do we still need paid ads?**

Ads can help with time-sensitive inventory, seasonal repairs, and testing offers. We use them alongside improvements to the pages customers can keep finding in organic search.

**When should we expect to see results?**

Timing varies by site, market, and when changes go live. We look for early changes in discovery, indexing, and impressions, then compare enquiries, bookings, or orders over a longer period.

**What will the free automotive SEO audit cover?**

We start with your website, target locations, and business goals. We identify priority issues on relevant inventory, service, location, or parts pages and explain what to fix first.
