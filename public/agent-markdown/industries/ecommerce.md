## Frequentlyasked questions

### What online retailers and product brands ask us

Get answers about product categories, product comparisons, buying guides and what we review in a free audit.

**What does Ecommerce SEO cover?**

We review technical SEO, your most important pages, and how online shoppers move from search to a next step.

**Can you help people find our product categories offering?**

Yes. We map relevant searches to a dedicated page and make sure visitors can see product specifications, fit details, stock, delivery, and returns.

**Should product comparisons have its own page?**

We start by reviewing whether customers search for it separately and whether you have enough useful information to justify a dedicated page.

**How do you approach content for buying guides?**

We start with practical buyer questions, including which product fits their needs and when it can arrive, and link the answers to the most relevant page.

**Can you improve visibility for store pages and pickup locations where available?**

We review your existing pages, service coverage, and applicable profiles. We build location or market pages when they can provide distinct information.

**What should visitors see before they choose a product and check out?**

Make product specifications, fit details, stock, delivery, and returns easy to find. We also check whether the next step works clearly on a phone.

**Can paid search support our product categories campaign?**

Yes, when you have a clear goal, a relevant page, and a budget you can measure. We use the results alongside organic search data.

**How do you measure whether our ecommerce SEO is working?**

We compare search visibility and visits with tracked actions such as enquiries, calls, orders, or bookings, depending on what your website can measure.

**Can we keep our current website and marketing team?**

Yes. We identify changes your team can make, changes that need developer access, and where a redesign could improve the experience.

**What will the free ecommerce SEO audit review?**

We start with product categories, product comparisons, and your target markets. We identify the issues and opportunities worth reviewing first.
